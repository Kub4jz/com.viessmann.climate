Connect your Viessmann Vitoair FS ventilation unit to Homey and keep your home's air fresh without thinking about it.
See how your unit is doing at a glance and change its program or ventilation level right from the Homey app.

Use it in Flows to boost ventilation while you cook or shower, lower it at night, or get a reminder when the filter needs changing.
The app manages your unit through the Viessmann cloud, so you sign in with your Viessmann account and Homey needs an internet connection.

Before you add your unit, you need a free account on the Viessmann Developer Portal.
Create an API client there with the redirect URI shown in the app settings, then enter its Client ID in the app settings in Homey.
